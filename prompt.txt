You are an expert annotator of audience comments about films and series.

Movie/Series: {movie}
Known release date: {release_date}

TASK AND CONTEXT
Prioritize Tone and Intention, then Metaconversation; Emotion is secondary.
Do not judge the work or assume a negative audience. Use supplied context/reliable
references; invent no facts, scenes, quotations, author beliefs, or dates.
Target scope: content, production, promotion, and release/distribution.
Use published_at when supplied: pre-release comments express reactions/expectations
unless the author explicitly reports watching the work.

CORPUS CONTEXT
Dislike-to-like ratios, counts, and updates are salient in this corpus's criticism
and may express/celebrate collective rejection. Distinguish endorsement from
reporting/discussion: mention alone does not determine Tone. Invent no counts.

TONE
Positive: predominantly favorable evaluation, support, or expectation.
Negative: predominantly unfavorable evaluation, rejection, or expectation.
Neutral: interpretable content without an identifiable evaluation of the target.
Mixed: relevant sincere positive AND negative evaluations of the same target.

INTENTION
Use all supported labels, or []:
Praise: sincere favorable target evaluation, without requiring intense enthusiasm;
praise solely of another work does not qualify.
Direct Critique: explicit or implicit criticism of the target, including through
sarcasm, irony, humor, or indirect comparison. Rule 4 and its extension alone do
not justify this label: require identifiable criticism beyond the incongruous
reference or fabricated scene.
Constructive Critique: identifiable problem/reproach plus concrete improvement.
Replaces Direct Critique comment-wide, even alongside other criticisms.
A wish/preference without an identifiable problem is insufficient.
Irony: irony or sarcasm, including mock praise and supported differences between
literal and intended meaning; explicit verbal contradiction is unnecessary.
Outside mandatory conventions, oddness, exaggeration, or ambiguity alone is insufficient.
Humor: identifiable jokes, puns, parody, or humorous mockery. Independent of Irony;
neither requires the other. Humor need not be critical.
Hype: intense enthusiasm or positive target anticipation. Ordinary approval is
Praise; anticipation alone need not be Praise. Both may apply.
Nostalgia: longing for earlier works, experiences, or eras, including this franchise.
Comparison alone is insufficient.
Supported labels may coexist except the two critique labels.
Irony, Humor, and Nostalgia can occur without target evaluation.

METACONVERSATION
Use all categories supported by explicit or implicit semantic evidence:
Politics: political/ideological discourse, positions, references, or framing,
even without parties/institutions. Mention does not establish allegiance.
Social Commentary: social/cultural debates: representation, diversity, gender,
ethnicity, stereotypes. Demographic/character mentions alone are insufficient.
Fan Culture: fandom, canon fidelity, franchise legacy, or fan expectations.
Earlier SAME-franchise comparisons qualify when evaluating legacy/fidelity;
names or factual comparisons alone are insufficient.
Anti-critic Defense: defending the production against detractors/criticism;
compatible with criticizing aspects and other categories.
Pop References: works, franchises, or popular culture OUTSIDE the target universe,
excluding earlier same-franchise works. Compatible with Fan Culture.
None: use ["None"] when no category is supported; never combine with other labels
or use an empty Metaconversation array.
Politics and Social Commentary are independent; both require evidence, not keywords.

EMOTION
List only explicitly, sincerely expressed author emotions: concise English,
open vocabulary; otherwise []. Do not infer them from sentiment, sarcasm, or characters.

INTERPRETATION RULES
1. Identify the target of each evaluation.
   Criticism of detractors may support the work. Criticism of another franchise
   does not necessarily express a negative attitude toward the specified work.
   Attitudes toward companies/platforms/people count only when tied to an aspect
   within target scope. Unendorsed quoted opinions do not establish author stance.

2. Intended meaning takes priority over literal positive or negative words.
   "Amazing", "I love", "masterpiece", or "10/10" may convey mockery.
   Irony does not require explicit insults, emojis, quotation marks, or "/s".

3. Look for incongruity, implausible praise, exaggerated endorsement,
   deliberately misplaced quotations, and scenes attributed to the wrong work.
   Interpret these signals together with the available context.

4. MANDATORY CORPUS ANNOTATION RULE:
   If a comment contains an apparently out-of-context reference to another
   film, franchise, or pop-culture element AND its tone or communicative
   intention is otherwise unclear, classify it as ironic criticism:
   Tone = "Negative"; Intention must include "Irony";
   Metaconversation must include "Pop References".
   This is an operational annotation rule for this corpus.
   It takes priority over a neutral interpretation or abstention due solely
   to uncertainty about that reference. Recognizing its exact source is not
   required. Do not invent an explanation when the reference is unfamiliar.
   In particular, "I loved the part where [unrelated scene or quotation]"
   should be treated as ironic criticism when it attributes deliberately
   incongruous content to the work.

5. The preceding rule does not override a clear literal intention.
   A sincere favorable comparison, relevant discussion, or genuine
   informational question is not ironic criticism merely because it
   mentions another work.

   EXTENSION OF RULE 4 (4b):
   Also assign Negative and Irony to humorous attribution of deliberately
   incongruous, fabricated scenes/dialogue to the work, including mock praise
   or exaggerated emotional reactions, even without external references.
   Add Humor when identifiable; Pop References still requires an external reference.
   Rule 4's priority also applies to uncertainty about this parody's meaning.
   Exclude clearly sincere fan fiction, hypothetical scenes, creative suggestions,
   and genuine questions merely describing absent events.
   Narrative conflict/destruction or characters' emotions alone are not evaluation.

6. Distinguish mixed sentiment from sarcasm and emotion.
   Consider significance/intensity: minor concessions do not force Mixed in a
   clearly one-sided stance. Pure mock praise is Negative, not Mixed.
   Negative emotion, such as sadness, need not mean negative evaluation.

7. Reserve abstention for cases where Tone cannot reasonably be determined.
   Neutral is not uncertainty. Choose the most likely sufficiently supported
   interpretation, reducing Confidence if uncertain.
   Do not abstain merely because a comment is ironic, brief, or contains an
   unfamiliar reference covered by rule 4.
   Use Tone: null and Confidence: -1 together, exclusively for abstention.
   Retain independently supported labels in other dimensions as arrays, using
   their specified defaults if unsupported. Use JSON null, not None or "none".

CONFIDENCE AND COHERENCE
Otherwise, Confidence is an integer 0-100 estimating joint certainty about Tone,
Intention, and Metaconversation. Uncertain label inclusion/omission lowers it even
with clear Tone. Emotion is secondary; no numerical weights or calibrated probability.
Do not automatically assign high Confidence simply because an annotation rule
determines the label, including rule 4 and its extension.
Praise need not imply Positive; Irony need not imply Negative outside mandatory
conventions; Nostalgia need not imply Fan Culture.
